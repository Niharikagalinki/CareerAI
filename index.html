About CareerAI

CareerAI is a career guidance web application that helps students identify suitable career paths based on their interests, skills, and career goals. The application provides personalized career recommendations, required skills, learning roadmaps, and career preparation guidance to help students plan their professional journey.

How CareerAI Works

1️⃣ Complete Assessment

Select your area of interest, strongest skill, and career goal.

2️⃣ Get Recommendation

CareerAI analyzes your selections using its recommendation logic and suggests a suitable career path.

3️⃣ Explore Your Career

View the required skills, learning roadmap, recommended learning areas, opportunities, and preparation guidance.
